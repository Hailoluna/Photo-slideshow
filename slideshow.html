Playing a Google Photos album (Windows)
  1. In Google Photos, open the album, click Share, then Create link or Copy link.
  2. Keep slideshow.html, sync-google-photos.ps1 and Start Slideshow.cmd together in one folder.
  3. Double-click Start Slideshow.cmd. The first time, it asks for the album link. It then downloads new photos into the Photos folder and opens the slideshow full screen. Press Alt+F4 to close it.

Each later start downloads only what's new, and removes photos you've taken out of the album. With no internet, it just plays what's already downloaded. To add more albums, put one link per line in albums.txt.

Getting your photos out of Google Drive
  1. Download a folder (simplest). On drive.google.com, right-click the folder and choose Download. Drive packs it into a .zip, and very large folders come as several .zip files. Click Choose files or .zip above and select all of them. You don't need to unzip anything.
  2. Stay in sync (Google Drive for desktop). Install Google Drive for desktop, right-click your photo folder, and choose Offline access → Available offline. Without this step the files only stream and won't play when you're offline. Then click Choose folder and pick the folder from your Google Drive (the G: drive on Windows, or Google Drive in Finder on a Mac).
  3. Phone or tablet. In the Drive app, select the photos and choose Download (Android) or Send a copy → Save to Files (iPhone/iPad). Then use Choose files or .zip.

Browsers can't reopen a folder on their own, so you'll pick it again each time you open this page. iPhone photos saved as HEIC only show in Safari. Other browsers skip them and say how many were skipped.

Keyboard and touch controls
  • Space pause / play · ← → previous / next
  • F full screen · M sound on/off · I file name on/off
  • Esc back to this screen (press it twice when in full screen)
  • Alt+F4 closes the window opened by Start Slideshow.cmd
  • Touch: swipe left or right to change photo, and tap to show the controls
  • Move the mouse to show the controls. They hide again after a few seconds.
